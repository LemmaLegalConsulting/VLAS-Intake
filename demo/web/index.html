Why Pipecat?

No software licensing fees.

Pipecat is a free, open-source framework for building maintainable AI voice tools that connect to the web or a phone line. It gives us the flexibility to handle legal intakes while keeping costs under our control.

We pay for the services we use—AI, speech, phone calls, and hosting—and the work to build and maintain the intake. The Pipecat framework itself has no licensing fee.

Legal-intake integration & support budget

Every option needs your screening rules, a classifier such as our simplified FETCH, and a tested LegalServer connection. The defaults budget for hosting, separate classification calls, maintenance, and setup. Bland, Telnyx, and Dialpad also include extra work to connect their conversation engine to our backend. These are editable planning assumptions, not vendor or Lemma Legal quotes.

Planning basis: $0.03/call for classification; $25/month in Pipecat supporting services or $50/month for a hosted platform’s separate backend; 4 maintenance hours/month at $150/hour. Hosted platforms add 2 upkeep hours/month. Setup assumes 40 hours to adapt our existing intake, plus 20 hours for a hosted-platform connection. This compares reuse of our existing code, not new builds from scratch.
