What the intake covers

  1. Establish the basics

    Ask how to reach the caller and where they need help.

  2. Understand the legal problem

    Let callers explain what happened in their own words, with follow-up questions to clarify what they need.

  3. Screen the household

    Gather household and financial details to check the program’s eligibility rules and identify referral needs.

  4. Prepare the handoff

    Organize the caller’s story and screening details for staff review in LegalServer.

The 300% poverty-guideline screen is specific to this project. Programs must validate their own rules and reference data.

03 · SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

One intake engine. Two entry points.

Browser and telephone intake share a Pipecat engine; each has its own deployment.

Deployment and turn-taking details

FastAPI and TypeScript run on one Fly Machine (2 shared CPUs, 2 GB RAM). Sessions stay in memory, last up to ten minutes, and are limited to two concurrent conversations.

Voice activity detection, Smart Turn, and echo cancellation support natural turn-taking. Recognition and speaker-echo errors remain possible.

Keeping the machine warm avoids startup delay. Speech recognition, turn detection, model generation, and synthesis still add latency; performance is not benchmarked.

Other branches explore Deepgram / Flux and Cartesia. Switching providers requires migration and testing.

ILLUSTRATIVE REQUEST EXCERPT

Create the matter

POST /api/v2/matters
{
  "first_name": "Alex",
  "last_name": "Parker",
  "legal_problem_code": "63",
  "county_of_dispute": {
    "county_FIPS": "51031"
  },
  "number_of_adults": 2,
  "number_of_children": 1,
  "income_eligible": true
}

Fictional excerpt; not a complete request.

  1. Create & validate

    Build a typed payload and POST to /matters. A successful response must include a matter UUID.

  2. Attach structured records

    POST to /matters/{uuid}/incomes, /adverse_parties, and /additional_names as applicable.

  3. Add the narrative

    Attach the narrative, assets, and applicable rejection notes at /matters/{uuid}/notes.

Staff review is still required. Failed child writes can leave an incomplete matter; there is no transactional queue. Validate lookup fields and rejection rules for the receiving site. An unfinished intake without an address currently maps to “Other” rejection.
Conflict checking: what is real, what is illustrated?

The intake collects adverse parties. This demo checks two invented records, not LegalServer’s database. Production conflict checks require authorized data and staff review.
