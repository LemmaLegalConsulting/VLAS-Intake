What you get out of the box

Four ways to build the first conversation.

Pipecat

A free, open-source framework. Connect speech, AI models, and web or phone audio with code you control. Choose and pay for providers and hosting separately.

Our role: build and operate the intake application, including its interface and integrations.

Framework & license ↗

Bland

Hosted voice agents, speech and language models, a conversation builder, and knowledge bases. Tools, webhooks, and code steps can connect the conversation to other systems.

Still to build: legal screening, classification, and the LegalServer workflow. Custom services may be needed; a separate server is not mandatory for every integration.

Integration tools ↗

Telnyx

Hosted voice agents with speech services, phone connectivity, and a configuration dashboard. Webhook tools can send information to external services and receive results during a call.

Still to build: legal screening, classification, and the LegalServer workflow. Telnyx can support these connections; they need custom configuration and testing.

Assistant tools ↗

Dialpad

Autonomous voice and chat agents, handoffs to staff, connectors, and conversation analytics within Dialpad’s platform.

Still to build: intake-specific rules and classification. Confirm the connector and plan needed for your LegalServer workflow with Dialpad before committing.

The work that remains

Completed intake is the deliverable.

These are implementation tasks for every platform, including Pipecat. A built-in tool connector does not supply the legal-aid workflow.

Identify the legal problem

Our intake uses a simplified version of FETCH, also built by Lemma Legal, to classify the caller’s problem and guide follow-up questions. Configure and test the categories your program uses.

Apply the program’s rules

Build the service-area, household, income, and referral checks. Keep them current as eligibility rules change.

Complete the LegalServer intake

Map answers to your fields, authorize the connection, validate submissions, and handle failed or repeated requests. Include the caller’s story and screening results for staff review.

Keep it working

Test English and Spanish conversations, review mistakes, monitor connections, and update the intake as staff needs change.

For Bland or Telnyx, a custom service can carry the classifier and LegalServer logic. Some steps can run in the platform itself. The right split depends on the workflow; an extra API service is a design choice, not a universal platform fee.
